Place your profile photo here named **profile.jpg** (or profile.png).

1. The Hero section (circular morphing frame on the right side)
2. The About section (rectangular frame on the left side)

Recommended image specs:
- Size: at least 400×500px
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Aspect ratio: roughly portrait (3:4 or square)
- File name: profile.jpg
